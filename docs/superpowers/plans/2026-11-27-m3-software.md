# Milestone 3 — Software & GoFreight Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let the player run the company on software and upgrades and feel it in the numbers. A growing company without an FMS drowns in spreadsheets, rival vendors sell an FMS and an RMS from day one, GoFreight launches its FMS, RMS and AI partway through the run, switching vendors has a real short-term cost, and 19 upgrades (single items, tracks, timed items and policies) trade money or a lever for a gain.

**Architecture:** One shared effects list (§11 "How effects work"). A data catalog (`src/rules/catalog.ts`) describes each product, upgrade, policy and temporary state as effects: one lever, a scope (company, office, or a department of an office, optionally narrowed to the owner or to some task kinds), an amount and a duration. Each day `foldEffects` adds up whatever is active into one table, and every rule that reads a lever reads it through that table. The save stores what was bought and when; effects are always worked out again. Save format goes to version 3.

**Tech Stack:** Unchanged from M2 (Vite 8.3.5, React 19.3.1, TypeScript 7.0.2, Vitest 5.0.5, Playwright 1.64.1, Node 24). No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-ff-tycoon-design.md`: all of §11 except the customer portal, and the M3 lines in §1, §5 (effects on workload, speed and capacity), §7 (RMS), §12 (rotating offers are *not* M3), §14 (costs, Money panel, year report), §15 (no company value for purchases), §16 (where things are bought, auto-pause) and §19. **(owner)** items are fixed. The plan cites §11's tables rather than repeating them. Scope issue: #11.

**Tracking:** GitHub issue #11. Branch `11-m3-software`. End every commit message with `Refs #11`. The PR says `Closes #11`.

**Execution:** subagent-driven, Opus subagents. Each task ends "ready for review" and gets a Codex review before a task that depends on it starts. Don't start until the owner says go.

## Scope

**In scope (#11):** everything in §11 except the customer portal:
- the software lineup, vendor drift (Monolith's price, LegacyFreight's outage days), contracts, renewal, switching costs, cut-over, dependent products ending together, new offices joining subscriptions;
- growing without an FMS: spreadsheet overhead and lost paperwork;
- GoFreight's launches on a seeded schedule with launch cards, and the "While you were away" card plus the 90-day ramp for M2 saves;
- the 19 upgrades with their shapes, places, costs and unlocks;
- software and upgrade costs in money, cash flow, year report and end screen; none in company value;
- save format version 3.

**Not in scope:** the customer portal, Pricing, Customer Service, satisfaction (#10); rotating upgrade offers, market events, the news ticker and the mode picker (#13). Every game is Normal until M6, but the rules take the mode, so Easy's "GoFreight from the start" is built and tested.

## Global Constraints

- **Everything in AGENTS.md:**
  - The rules stay pure; randomness comes only from the seeded RNG. Folding draws no random numbers. Launch dates come from their own stream derived from the seed (like `pickStarterPorts`), so they never move the main stream.
  - Every number lives in `src/rules/balance.ts`. `catalog.ts` holds structure only (ids, shape, where, lever, scope) and reads every amount from `B`.
  - All player text goes through `t()`, in `en`, `zh-Hant` and `zh-Hans`. Chinese wording follows `docs/i18n/zh-glossary.md`.
  - CSS color tokens only, light and dark; no horizontal page scroll at 375 px.
- **Public repo:** rival vendors are fictional; never name or mock a real vendor. GoFreight appears by name. The overhead log line names "an FMS", never GoFreight (§11).
- **Who does what (§11):** software mainly raises capacity, AI mainly raises speed and shortens minimum durations, and no upgrade raises capacity or shortens a minimum duration. A test in Task 4 enforces the last rule on the catalog.
- **Fairness (§2):** a player who never buys software still finishes a run (Task 9).
- **Terms:** **task**, **workload**, **speed**, **capacity**, **minimum duration**, plus **software**, **subscription**, **upgrade**, **policy**, **cut-over**, **spreadsheet overhead**. Product and vendor names are proper names, the same in every language.
- **Profit always equals the change in cash** (§14). Subscriptions go in `software`; one-time upgrade prices, upgrade monthly costs, migration and early-exit fees go in `upgrades`. Lever-paid policies show where their lever does (Overtime in salaries).
- **Every price shown before a decision is what the decision charges.** Previews and rules call the same functions.
- **Save format:** `CURRENT_SAVE_VERSION = 3`, step `migrations[2]`, folder `saves/v3/`. v3 is unreleased until this PR merges, so Task 9 may delete and regenerate `saves/v3/`. Run `npx vitest --clearCache` after writing fixtures.
- **Dialogs** follow the M1 modal pattern. **Shortcuts** show in tooltips as "action (key)".
- Run `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` before every commit.

## Review Focus

1. **Switching mid-contract with almost enough cash.** The preview's migration and early-exit fees are exactly what the switch charges; one dollar short is refused and changes nothing; exactly enough succeeds, and the old product is billed once more at the month end. *Tests: Task 6.*
2. **Dropping GoFreight AI's last parent.** AI ends the same day with no fee, the preview says so, and the combo stops while either GoFreight product is missing. With only GoFreight RMS left, AI acts on Sales only. *Tests: Tasks 4 and 6.*
3. **An M2 save carried over.** It loads with no software, a "While you were away" card lists every product already launched, overhead ramps in over 90 days, and a save paused at a year end shows the report and the card one at a time. *Tests: Tasks 3, 5 and 7.*
4. **Levers moving under open tasks.** Capacity cut below what someone holds (Open-plan office, cancelling an FMS) drops and unassigns nothing. Workload rising with overhead never loses progress; workload falling below progress finishes the task that day. A lost task keeps its deadline. *Tests: Tasks 4 and 5.*
5. **A wide company.** With 23 offices every price, preview and money line fits on one line at 1280 px and 375 px in all three languages, and the Open office button shows "+$R / mo rent · +$S / mo software". *Tests: Tasks 12 and 13.*

### Task 4: The catalog and the effects fold

The core of the milestone. Every later rule plugs in here.

**Files:** `src/rules/catalog.ts`, `src/rules/effects.ts`, `src/rules/effects.test.ts`, `src/rules/balance.ts`, `src/rules/work.ts`, `src/rules/chain.ts`, `src/rules/deadlines.ts`, `src/rules/demand.ts`, `src/rules/handling.ts`, `src/rules/sales.ts`, `src/rules/staff.ts`, `src/rules/money.ts`, `src/rules/step.ts`, their tests

**Interfaces:**
- **Levers** (§11's list): `type Lever = 'speed' | 'capacity' | 'workload' | 'minDuration' | 'winChance' | 'newRequests' | 'repeatRequests' | 'margin' | 'deadline' | 'lateFee' | 'rent' | 'salary' | 'candidatesPerRoll' | 'rerollCost' | 'handlingRate'`.
- **`interface Effect { lever: Lever; op: 'add' | 'mul'; amount: number; depts?: readonly DeptKey[] | 'covered'; kinds?: readonly TaskKind[]; ownerOnly?: true; bothEndsOwn?: true }`.** `depts` missing means every department. `'covered'` is GoFreight AI's: the departments its acting GoFreight parents cover.
- **`type EffectSource = { kind: 'software'; productId: ProductId } | { kind: 'combo' } | { kind: 'cutover'; productId: ProductId } | { kind: 'overhead' } | { kind: 'outage' } | { kind: 'upgrade'; upgradeId: UpgradeId; level: number } | { kind: 'ramp' }`** (`ramp` never appears in the table; it only scales overhead).
- **`interface ActiveEffect extends Effect { source: EffectSource; officeId: string | null; depts: readonly DeptKey[]; untilDay: number | null }`** (`officeId` null is company-wide; `untilDay` the last played day it acts, null while owned).
- **How effects combine** (§11 leaves it to the builder): for one lever at one place, adds sum and multipliers multiply, applied as `(base + add) × mul`, then the lever's floor from `B.effects`. Predictable, order-free, and the screen shows the net.
- **`interface EffectContext { officeId: string; dept: DeptKey; kind?: TaskKind; isOwner?: boolean; bothEndsOwn?: boolean }`** and **`interface EffectTable { day: number; effects: readonly ActiveEffect[]; mod(lever: Lever, ctx: EffectContext): { add: number; mul: number } }`**. `mod` matches office (or company), department, and the optional filters.
- **`catalog.ts`:** `interface Product { id; category; maker: 'rival' | 'gofreight'; name: string; covers: readonly DeptKey[]; effects: readonly Effect[] }` (RMS `covers: ['sales']`; M4 adds `'pricing'`) and `interface Upgrade { id; shape: 'single' | 'track' | 'timed' | 'policy'; where: 'office' | 'dept' | 'company' | 'hiring'; dept?: DeptKey; levels: number; days?: number; unlockOffices?: number; effects: readonly (readonly Effect[])[] }` (effects per level). `PRODUCTS`, `UPGRADES`; names from Task 1.
- **`foldEffects(s: GameState, day: number, today: { outage?: boolean } = {}): EffectTable`** collects for played day `day`:
  - each acting subscription's product effects, company-wide;
  - `combo` (margin, and invoice workload, `kinds: ['invoice']`) while GoFreight FMS and RMS both act;
  - `cutover` while `day <= startDay + days`, with `days` = `switchDays` if `replaces` else `firstDays`, speed on the product's departments;
  - `overhead` per office with no FMS acting: a workload add on every task there. Task 4 leaves the source returning 0; Task 5 fills it with `overheadAdd`, which includes the ramp;
  - `outage` when `today.outage`: Ops speed ×0, company-wide;
  - each owned upgrade acting on `day`: a single item from `startDay + 1`; a track's highest level owned (not the sum); a timed item until `startDay + days`; a policy until its `endDay`; office items scoped to their office (and `dept` for header items).
- **`nextDayEffects(s)`** = `foldEffects(s, s.day + 1)`: what the next played day uses; the UI shows this, so a purchase shows at once.
- **Readers** (the only way a lever is read):
  - `speedOf(fx, e)`, `capacityOf(fx, e)` (owner filter from `e.isOwner`);
  - `workloadOf(fx, task, shipment)` and `minDurationOf(fx, task)`, **read each day**: a task stores its base workload and progress compares with the effective one, so an FMS bought today lifts overhead from open tasks too;
